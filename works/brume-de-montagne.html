02/2026 · Orchestral work

Brume de montagne et lumière sur une île humide

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

This work depicts the misty, drizzly atmosphere of Taiwan's mountain regions from early morning through the afternoon. Humid air spreads throughout the hills, with light gradually emerging through the fog. By the afternoon, sunlight warms the landscape and the rain temporarily ceases, revealing the scent of vegetation and flora. As evening approaches, light drizzle returns, leaving the mountains with a subtle sense of breathing and echo.

The piece employs various extended techniques to evoke the dampness of the environment, conveying a slightly uneasy yet lively and vibrant sense of Taiwan's natural landscape.

山霧與濕島之光

這首樂曲描寫台灣山區從清晨延續至午後的微雨狀態。潮濕的空氣瀰漫於山間，光影在霧氣中緩慢顯現。午後，陽光漸暖，雨暫時止歇，花草與植被的氣息隨之浮現。入夜前，細雨再起，山間留下若有似無的呼吸與回聲。

作品運用多種特殊演奏技巧，使音樂呈現出潮濕、略帶不適卻充滿生機與活力的台灣景觀。
