03/2026 · Orchestral study (string parts)

Fugue in Motion for Orchestra (String Part)

String orchestra. The full orchestral version will be released at a later time.

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

Fugue in Motion is a study for orchestra. The string parts form the structural backbone of the full composition. The idea of a fugue is subtly embedded within a contemporary musical language. Rather than presenting a traditional fugue explicitly, the musical figures unfold as a continuous, flowing texture. The full orchestral version will be released at a later time.

賦格的律動

《Fugue in Motion》是一首管弦樂練習作品。弦樂部分構成整首作品的結構骨架。賦格的概念被巧妙地融入當代音樂語法之中。音樂聲響以連續流動的方式展開，而非呈現傳統賦格的旋律形式。全管弦樂版本將於稍後發佈。
