Mosquito Concerto

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

Summer has arrived. Have you drawn a cross on your arm with your fingernails?

It is a small ritual of the season. The air is humid, the light dim, the world seemingly still—yet a thin, persistent high frequency begins to circle near the ear. Its presence is insignificant in size, yet powerful enough to command the entire night.

This concerto is built upon the idea of confrontation. Extremely high string tremolos and glissandi trace the erratic flight paths of the mosquito, hovering at the edge of perception. Fragmented woodwind gestures and sudden brass intrusions suggest abrupt approaches and near attacks. Percussive strikes mirror the instant of the clap—decisive, urgent, though not always victorious.

The music oscillates between tension and suspension. Each apparent silence risks becoming merely the preparation for another return. The listener is drawn into a microscopic battlefield: the adversary is small, yet impossible to ignore. The drama here is not monumental, but sensory—an intrusion that destabilizes stillness.

"Mosquito Concerto" is not merely a humorous depiction of a summer nuisance. It transforms an intimate bodily experience into a study of sonic friction and temporal suspense. The cross drawn upon the skin is both a gesture of relief and a symbolic act of resistance—an individual's response to the nearly invisible.

When the final strike falls, the night may seem calm once more. Yet somewhere in the distance, a faint high tone lingers—reminding us that this encounter never fully ends.

蚊子協奏曲

夏天到了，你用指甲在手臂上畫十字了嗎？

那是一個幾乎每個人都熟悉的夜晚儀式。燈光昏黃，空氣濕熱，萬物靜止，卻總有一個極細微的高頻在耳邊盤旋。牠的存在微不足道，卻足以支配整個夜晚的神經。

本曲以「對抗」為核心結構。弦樂極高音域的顫動與滑音構成蚊子盤旋的聲響軌跡，在空間中若即若離；木管斷續的呼吸與銅管突刺象徵忽然逼近的威脅；打擊的瞬間爆發，則如同拍掌落下的剎那——精準、急迫，卻未必成功。

音樂在緊張與靜止之間反覆擺盪。每一次看似終結的停頓，都可能只是下一輪盤旋的開始。聽者彷彿置身於一場微觀戰場：對手極小，卻無法忽視；敵意並非宏大敘事，而是一種持續干擾感官的存在。

《蚊子協奏曲》並非單純的幽默描寫，而是將夏夜裡最日常、最瑣碎的身體經驗，轉化為聲響張力與時間感的拉鋸。那條在手臂上畫下的十字，既是止癢的動作，也是對抗的象徵——一個人在黑暗中，對不可見之物所作出的回應。

當最後一聲拍擊落下，我們或許以為夜晚歸於平靜；然而在極遠處，仍隱約殘留著一線高頻——提醒著這場戰鬥從未真正結束。
