Switzerland: Preview Mode瑞士：預覽模式

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

🎧 於 SoundCloud 收聽

Lately, my feeds have been filled with images of Swiss travel destinations. Although I have been to Switzerland, I have never seen its legendary alpine landscapes in person. Rather than describing the Alps as a fairy-tale paradise, this piece is more a record of a daydream. I find myself imagining a journey to that seemingly heavenly place—an almost unreal landscape where worries dissolve into clear air and distant peaks.

Switzerland: Preview Mode is not a portrayal of Switzerland itself, but a playful and slightly self-aware glimpse of longing: a musical preview of mountains I have yet to encounter.

最近常常被推播各種瑞士旅遊景點的畫面。雖然我去過瑞士，卻從未親眼見過傳說中如仙境般的阿爾卑斯山風景。與其說這首作品是在描繪那片壯麗山景，不如說它更像是一段白日夢的紀錄——我幻想自己走向那個宛如人間天堂的地方，一個彷彿沒有煩惱、空氣清澈、群峰遙遠而寧靜的世界。

《瑞士：預覽模式》並不是對真實風景的再現，而是一種帶著幽默與自覺的預覽：在尚未真正抵達之前，先用音樂想像那片未曾親見的山。
