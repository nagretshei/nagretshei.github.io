10/2021 · Immersive electroacoustic music (binaural) 10/2021 · 沉浸式電聲音樂（雙耳錄音）

Summer in Trees樹林裡的夏日

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

🎧 於 SoundCloud 收聽

The music depicts beautiful vivid green trees in summer, with trembling of leaves and glint of sunshine between leaves.

The music is in binaural format. So it is best to listen to it with headphones, but stereo speakers are okay, too.

Love it? The work is also available as an NFT on OpenSea.

這首音樂描繪夏日裡翠綠盎然的樹林，葉片輕輕顫動，陽光在葉隙間閃爍。

音樂為雙耳（binaural）格式，建議以耳機聆聽為佳，一般立體聲喇叭亦可。

喜歡這首作品嗎？也能在 OpenSea 上以 NFT 形式收藏。
