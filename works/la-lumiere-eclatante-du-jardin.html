La lumière éclatante du jardin花園裡的艷陽

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

🎧 於 SoundCloud 收聽

This piece depicts a garden filled with flowers and sunlight.

Radiant light settles across the space, resting upon clusters of blossoms.

Colors unfold in layered groupings, forming a gently flowing sense of joy.

這首樂曲描繪一座充滿花朵與陽光的花園。艷陽灑落其間，光線停留在花叢之上，各色花朵一叢一叢地綻放，形成一種靜靜流動的喜悅。
