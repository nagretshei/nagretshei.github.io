02/2026 · Orchestral work

La Mer pour orchestre

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

The work opens with strings depicting urgent and forceful waves, their concentrated energy striking the shoreline. The music then rapidly subsides as the tide recedes; sea winds pass through rocky caverns, leaving behind long, whistle-like resonances. From the shore, ship horns sound and sails are raised, setting off across the calm sea and gradually moving toward a distant horizon of aspiration.

海

作品開端以弦樂刻畫急促而猛烈的浪潮，密集的能量拍擊岸線。隨後音樂迅速收斂，潮汐退卻，海風穿行於岩洞之間，留下如哨音般悠長的殘響。岸邊船隻鳴笛，帆影升起，在平穩的海面上航行，逐漸駛向夢想的遠方。
