Alpsolve雪峰融境

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

🎧 於 SoundCloud 收聽

This orchestral work unfolds in the high regions of the Alps—snow-veiled peaks rising one after another, an unbroken procession of white.

In such vastness, in such piercing cold, the thought of oneself begins to loosen and fall away. Private emotions, personal narratives, the small urgencies of daily life—before this immensity, they appear almost weightless, almost worldly.

The music turns away from inherited orchestral conventions and from the security of formal design. Amid grandeur and extreme cold, structure feels secondary, even unnecessary.

Instead, the sound dwells in delicate high frequencies—fine, glacial threads of tone that evoke the sharpness of frozen air and the brightness of snow under an austere sky.

Silence is allowed to remain. Through these openings, one senses the thin alpine atmosphere, the stillness that does not merely surround you but quietly enters you.

Near the end, a mountain lake emerges—its surface trembling with reflected light. While composing, I found myself no longer observing it, but dissolving into it.

Reason suggested that the music should return—that it should gather its theme once more, fulfill expectation, close its arc. Yet every addition felt excessive, almost coarse, as if something unnecessary were being placed upon what was already complete.

Nature does not submit to form. It cannot be contained within structure, nor shaped according to the architecture of human thought. It exists beyond intention, beyond design. And so the music does not return. It does not resolve. It allows the self to vanish into the lake.

And yet—

These towering mountains, for all their magnificence, may be more fragile than we imagine.

Perhaps these fields of snow, so luminous and seemingly eternal, will one day quietly, almost imperceptibly, begin to disappear.

這首管弦樂在描繪在阿爾卑斯山高山區的景色，靄靄白雪覆蓋的尖山頂，一座連綿一座。

這首樂曲避開了傳統所期待的配器法則以及曲式，因為我認為在這樣雄偉的自然景色和極端的低溫中，任何人都很難再想著自己，任何關於私人的情緒或生活，在這樣的大自然景色中，無不顯得世俗和渺小了。

樂曲採用的聲音為細緻的高頻，用來引發寒冷的低溫和景色。多處的留白，讓人感受到當下稀薄的空氣與寧靜。

在曲末在樂曲近結束段，描繪了一片山中的湖水，波光粼粼，自己在創作時，已經不由得消失於其中，更奇妙的是，雖然理智上有考慮應該讓樂曲回到主題，以切合傳統的期待，但是發現，任何添加都讓樂曲更顯得俗氣了，彷彿在說，大自然是無法被任何「曲式」或「結構」框架住的，而大自然的景色，也從不會依照我們人類的「文明思維」來設計，他是超越人類的思維的。所幸，就讓「自己」消失在那片湖水中吧！

最後，這些宏偉的高山，看似壯闊，但他可能也比你想像的還脆弱，也許，這些雪景，將在不知不覺中漸漸消失⋯⋯
