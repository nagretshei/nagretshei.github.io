First Contemporary Classical AI Composer Robot Project

AI music generation system that produced 62 works, nicknamed “Little Pi.”

The inspiring story of "Little Pi" is a tale of a music composition robot powered by AI. Developed by a team of three including Cheng-Yu Wu, Chia-Hsuan Tsai, and Zoe Lin, Little Pi, which is a real AI system, has recently made significant progress and is now ready to showcase its abilities to the world.

In the video, viewers can see Little Pi's diary of performances, which showcases its capabilities in AI music generation. Utilizing a transformer model, the robot is able to generate music (and accompanying visuals generated entirely by AI model) without any human intervention. The team behind Little Pi has also included their own understanding of contemporary music in the robot's compositions, resulting in a unique and captivating experience.

The system/project is developed by a team of three:

  • Zoe (Yi-Cheng) Lin — composer and main developer
  • Cheng-Yu Wu — flutist / project planner
  • Chia-Hsuan Tsai — harpsichord / flute player / project planner
