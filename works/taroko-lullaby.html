Taroko: A Lullaby of Coastal

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

This work is about the relationship between people and nature.

A lullaby is a mother’s song to her child. It is gentle and warm. The child rests in her arms, rocked slowly back and forth, until the body and mind grow quiet and fall asleep. In this piece, the mother gradually becomes nature, and perhaps even something divine. She is hidden in the wind, sea, mountains, and stars. Though unseen, her presence can be felt as both freedom and a quiet embrace.

The music begins with the coastal wind at dawn. The wind sings through the tall cliffs of Taroko, its echoes traveling through the canyon, before moving toward the sea. Sunlight spreads across the water, warm and peaceful. The wind slowly enters the heart of the waves, and the sea grows stronger. At sunset, waves rise and break into spray. They become more than a picture of nature: they carry the overwhelming feeling I experience when confronted with the beauty of the evening sky.

At the height of the waves, I feel something like an encounter with the divine—a powerful yet reassuring force that shakes me from within, almost beyond what I can bear. Yet the wind does not leave the child there. It gradually calms and rocks the child back toward stillness. Beneath a sky full of stars, the remaining waves continue to rise and fall, carrying the listener gently into sleep.

This is not only a piece about wind and sea, but also a lullaby about being held by nature.

Taroko is one of Taiwan’s most iconic landscapes and part of my homeland. For me, it is more than a magnificent gorge; it is a place of familiarity and belonging, like a mother. The “mother” in this work may be nature itself, Taroko, or something greater hidden within them—a presence that gives us both freedom and shelter.

The music focuses on the “weight of wind“ and the “volume of the sea”. Though wind has no physical shape, its sound is formed into thick, flowing masses, like a mother’s hands wrapping around her child—the listener. The waves are given physical weight through spatial sound, moving around the listener, coming closer, pulling away, rising, and falling.

All of the wave sounds are digitally created. Using ChucK, white noise and algorithms build an imagined sea, allowing the sounds of nature to become part of a dialogue between the natural world and human imagination.

The beauty of Taroko was once interrupted by landslides caused by the 2024 earthquake, temporarily keeping people from entering the landscape. In a twenty-first century of melting glaciers, extreme weather, and a changing earth, perhaps we can pause and ask: “if nature is the mother in this lullaby, how might we respond to her care?”

太魯閣：海風的搖籃曲

這首作品描繪的是自然與人之間的關係。

「搖籃曲」是母親唱給孩子的歌，溫柔而安穩。孩子躺在母親懷裡，隨著搖曳的節奏，逐漸放下身心，進入夢鄉。對我而言，作品中的「母親」逐漸成為自然，甚至是某種「神」的象徵。祂藏在風、海、山谷與星空之中，以看似無形卻真實存在的力量環抱著人，使人在遼闊的天地裡感受到自由與呵護。

樂曲從清晨的海風開始。風如歌一般穿越高聳的太魯閣峽谷，回聲在山壁間迴盪，隨後吹向海面。陽光落在海上，波光粼粼，溫暖而寧靜。海風逐漸深入海浪中心，力量隨之增強；到了黃昏，彩霞映照海面，浪花四起。此時的海浪不只是自然景象，也像是面對晚霞之美時，內心受到的巨大衝擊。

當海浪將海風推向最高點，那一刻對我而言，甚至有一種初次面對「神」的感受：一股強大而安穩的力量席捲而來，內在卻同時受到劇烈震撼，幾乎難以承受。然而，海風終究捨不得孩子停留在這樣的衝擊之中。它逐漸放緩，再次搖曳著孩子，將他安穩地放下。最後，在星空形成的巨大搖籃之中，海浪餘波持續起伏，帶著聽眾逐漸沉入睡眠。

太魯閣是台灣最著名的自然景觀之一，也是我的家鄉。對我而言，它不只是壯麗的山谷，也是帶著親切感與歸屬感的地方，如同母親一般。因此，作品中的「母親」可以是自然、太魯閣，也可以是隱藏於自然之中的某種存在，給予人自由、安穩與呵護。

作品特別強調風的「重量」與海的「量體」。雖然風本身無形，音樂中的聲音筆觸卻呈現厚重而流動的團塊，如同母親的手，以具體的觸感環抱著孩子，也就是聽眾。海浪則透過空間處理，使聲音具有重量，在聽眾周圍靠近、退去、起伏。

全曲中的海浪聲皆為電腦模擬，使用 ChucK 以白噪音與程式生成的方式建構，使自然的聲音成為人與自然之間另一種想像性的連結。

太魯閣的美，曾因 2024 年地震造成的坍方而一度阻擋人們進入。當我們站在這片山海之間，面對冰川消融、極端氣候與地殼持續變動的 21 世紀，我們是否也該思考：如果自然是這首搖籃曲中的母親，我們又該如何回應這份呵護？
