02/2026 · Orchestral work

Joy Under the Falling Leaves

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

This orchestral work is composed using a relatively traditional orchestration, portraying a scene beneath withered branches and falling leaves, where the landscape reveals a quiet and subtle beauty. The appreciation of withered branches reflects an Eastern philosophical perspective, in which joy and sorrow are embraced simultaneously, leading the listener toward an inner state of contemplation and prayer. At the climax of the piece, a sorrowful, prayer-like passage emerges in the French horn, which ultimately resolves into a celebratory joy—an expression of profound and sincere love for life.

枯枝下的喜悅

這首管弦樂作品採用較為傳統的配器手法，描寫枯枝與落葉之下的景象，畫面寧靜而美妙。體會枯枝之美，是一種屬於東方的哲學視角——在其中，生命的喜悅與悲傷得以同時被包容與欣賞，並引領聆聽者進入內在的祈禱狀態。樂曲在發展至高潮時，法國號奏出帶有悲傷色彩的祈禱段落，隨後卻在最歡慶的喜悅中結束，象徵對生命深刻而真摯的熱愛。
