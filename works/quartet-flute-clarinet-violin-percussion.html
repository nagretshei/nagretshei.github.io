01/2026 · Chamber quartet

Quatuor pour flûte, clarinette, violon et percussion

Flute, clarinet, violin, and percussion. Performed via MIDI mockup.

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

I wrote a quartet for flute, clarinet, violin, and percussion as an exercise, using a MIDI mockup for the performance.

For listeners, very slow music like this can often create a more contemplative atmosphere, especially for those who enjoy contemporary music. For me, however, this kind of music is more difficult to use for storytelling or to evoke a particular visual landscape, which is why I have generally avoided writing in this style.

So I decided to treat this piece simply as an exercise and have some fun with it. I have not really explored this kind of musical language before. The only work of mine that comes somewhat close would probably be The Spirit of the Sacred Tree from 2022.

四重奏（長笛、豎笛、小提琴、打擊）

寫了首四重奏（為長笛、豎笛、小提琴、和打擊），當作練習，MIDI mockup 的演奏。

這種很慢很慢的音樂，對觀眾來說，如果是當代音樂的熱愛者，通常就會覺得可以把人帶入比較沉思的意境。對我來說，是比較難以敘事和呈現畫面景色，所以一直不大喜歡採用，所以當作個練習玩一玩，也沒什麼試過這種曲風（唯一比較貼近的應該是2022年的神木之靈吧）。
