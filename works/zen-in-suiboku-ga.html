04/2020 · Immersive electroacoustic music

Zen In Suiboku-ga

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

When I was composing this piece, I felt that I was painting a Suiboku-ga (Oriental Ink wash painting) with electronic music. There are mountains, trees, dried wood, rivers, waterfalls, birds, clouds, fogs, as well as the fisherman in the painting. Most of all, I depict my feeling of Zen of Suiboku-ga in the musical piece. Enjoy!
