01/2026 · Chamber trio (or four solo pieces) 01/2026 · 室內樂三重奏（或四首獨奏曲）

Pluie, routine et fatigue下雨天給社畜的厭世曲

Trio for marimba, harpsichord, and flute — or four independent solo pieces.

為馬林巴木琴、大鍵琴與長笛之三重奏，亦可拆成四首獨立的獨奏曲。

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

🎧 於 SoundCloud 收聽

Last night, thanks to insomnia (my head was full of music), I ended up writing a whole collection of "corporate fatigue" pieces today: a trio for marimba, harpsichord, and flute (Trio for Marimba, Harpsichord and Flute), or four solo pieces, each performed as an independent improvisation.

#Music for 21st-century Asian wage workers
#Overstimulated at midnight, mentally exhausted by morning

昨晚因為失眠（腦袋都是樂曲）所以今天寫了『厭世風』大集合的為馬林巴木琴、大鍵琴、和長笛的三重奏 (Trio for Marimba, Harpsichord and Flute)，由於這首樂曲創作過程是四個聲部各自完成再拼成一首，因此此曲可成為一首三重奏或四首獨奏。
