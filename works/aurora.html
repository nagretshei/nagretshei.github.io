07/2022 · Solo saxophone & multichannel immersive electroacoustics

3D Illusory Immersive Soundscape: Aurora

Recorded by Chun-Hao Ku, renowned Taiwanese saxophonist.

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

I love the photos in National Geographic magazine, especially those taken from the most remote places untouched by humans. I can imagine the photographers being immersed in those beautiful landscapes, feeling joy, peace, and perhaps loneliness while contemplating the insignificance of human beings on Earth.

I would like to convey such feelings and capture the breath of such landscapes through this work. The title, Aurora, intends to honor the sky, with magnificent mountains and coniferous forests blanketed in snow. I want my audience to see the reflections of the mountaintops on and in and ripples of surrounding lakes and feel what it is to breathe in cold and clean air. This landscape should feel joyous and untouched filled with love.

The work is written for a solo saxophone and electroacoustic performance. The solo part is mostly composed by extended techniques. The solo saxophone depicts mountains via multiphonics sounds: the aurora with practice tubes (plus glasses bottles); the ripples of lakes with singing-in-minor-seconds-while-playing techniques; and the lonely human with (only) quartertones during the solo passages. The electroacoustic part includes traditional chamber music, including the flute, piccolo, clarinet, bassoon, cello, and horn, as well as synthesized sounds. Together with those instrumental sounds, the solo saxophone part is seamlessly fused with the electroacoustic sounds.

The electroacoustic part is composed using the newest ambisonic techniques, with a 360-degree sound field surrounding the audience. When listening to this piece, audiences should use headphones to appreciate the binaural format.

極光

我喜歡國家地理雜誌上的照片，尤其是那些從人類未曾接觸過的最偏遠地區拍攝的照片。我可以想像攝影師們沉浸在那些美麗的風景中，在思考人類在地球上的渺小時，感到快樂、平靜，也許還有孤獨。

我想通過這幅作品傳達這樣的感受，捕捉這樣風景的氣息。極光這個標題意在向天空致敬，壯麗的山脈和被雪覆蓋的針葉林。我想讓我的觀眾看到山頂的倒影和周圍湖泊的漣漪，感受呼吸清新的冷空氣是什麼感覺。這片風景應該充滿愛意，充滿歡樂和原汁原味。

作品是為薩克斯管獨奏和電聲演奏而寫。獨奏部分主要由擴展技術組成。獨奏薩克斯管通過多聲部聲音描繪山脈：帶有練習管（加玻璃瓶）的極光；邊彈邊唱的湖水漣漪；在獨奏段落中，孤獨的人（只有）四分音。電聲部分包括長笛、短笛、單簧管、巴鬆管、大提琴、圓號等傳統室內樂，以及合成音色。薩克斯獨奏部分與那些器樂聲一起，與電聲聲音無縫融合。

電聲部分採用最新的立體聲技術組成，具有環繞聽眾的 360 度聲場。聽這首曲子時，觀眾應該戴上耳機來欣賞雙耳格式。
