11/2021 · Immersive electroacoustic music (Ambisonic)

Music from the Metaverse: The Spirit of the Giant Tree

🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

This is one of my series of auditory-visual synesthetic compositions, in which images are transformed into three-dimensional, immersive electroacoustic music.

Does a tree feel anything? What kind of consciousness does a tree have?

One day, I encountered a giant tree. Its rough bark and monumental trunk suddenly raised these questions in me. I wanted to search for an answer through music—to imagine what it might mean to experience the world from within a tree.

The Spirit of the Giant Tree depicts the life of a giant tree growing upon a massive rock. Through blooming, decay, illness, and eventual rebirth, the tree continuously directs its vitality toward growth and expansion, regardless of the environment in which it exists.

The work is composed in an ambisonic system. The audience is surrounded by sound representing the tree and experiences its journey from a first-person perspective, as though entering the center of the tree itself. Through immersive 3D sound, the listener is invited to sense not only the tree's growth and transformation, but also the primordial pulse of the forest and the earth.

During the compositional process, I suddenly realized that a giant tree might be understood as an extension of the Earth's consciousness—an embodiment of a life span far longer than that of a human being. When I close my eyes and listen to the music, I sense the ancient spirit of the Earth and the deep vitality of forests: a primordial throbbing, and a powerful impulse toward recovery even after the immense damage caused by humanity.

This realization became especially meaningful in the radically changing world of the twenty-first century. In the post-pandemic era, emerging technologies and the idea of the metaverse have generated excitement about new possibilities and new worlds, while at the same time, the ecological crisis has brought humanity a growing sense of uncertainty and even an eschatological imagination.

Yet the Earth possesses a vitality far beyond our own. It will continue to live and regenerate long after humanity—and perhaps other species she has brought forth—have passed away. The Earth gives life, and life inevitably comes and goes, while her capacity to nurture life endures.

Through the spirit of the giant tree, this work invites us to glimpse the ancient consciousness of the Earth and to experience, through sound, the mysterious power of its will to live.

Listening

For the most immersive experience, please listen with headphones in a quiet environment. Professional over-ear studio monitor headphones are recommended. Over-ear headphones generally provide a more immersive experience than in-ear headphones, although ordinary headphones can also be used. Even the wired Apple EarPods that were once included with iPhones can provide a suitable listening experience when connected to a computer.

Audience Response

“After listening to this work, I felt deeply healed. It felt as though I had entered the giant-tree forest in Avatar. It was breathtaking, and I wanted the experience to continue.”

巨木之靈

本作品為「聽像創作」系列之一，透過視覺與聽覺聯覺的創作方式，將影像轉化為三維、沉浸式的電聲音樂。

樹會感覺嗎？樹具有什麼樣的意識？

有一天，我看見一棵巨大的樹。祂粗糙的樹皮、龐大的樹幹，以及歷經歲月而形成的姿態，突然讓我產生了這些疑問。我想透過音樂尋找答案，試著想像：如果我們從一棵樹的內部感受世界，那會是什麼樣的經驗？

《巨木之靈》描繪一棵生長於巨岩之上的巨木，經歷生命中的開花、衰敗、疾病，以及最終的重生。無論身處何種環境，祂始終將所有的生命力投注於生長與延展之中。

作品以 Ambisonic 沉浸式聲音系統創作，讓聽眾被代表巨木的聲音包圍，彷彿進入巨木的中心，以第一人稱的視角參與祂的生命旅程。透過 3D 沉浸式聲音，聽眾不只是「聽見」一棵樹，而是被邀請去感受祂的生長、變化，以及原始森林與大地深處的悸動。

在創作過程中，我突然意識到：一棵巨大的樹，或許更像是地球意識的延伸——一種擁有遠遠超越人類生命尺度的存在。當我閉上眼睛聆聽這部作品時，我感受到森林原始而深沉的悸動，也彷彿觸碰到大地古老的精神，以及即使遭受人類巨大傷害，仍不斷尋求復原的強大生命意志。

這樣的體悟，在二十一世紀急遽變動的時代裡尤其深刻。後疫情時代與元宇宙、新科技的發展，使人們對新的世界與未來充滿期待；然而另一方面，地球生態危機也讓人類同時面對一種末世般的不安與想像。

但地球的生命力，或許遠遠超越我們所能想像的時間尺度。即使人類，以及其他由地球孕育而出的物種，最終都將離去，地球仍將持續存在、復原與孕育新的生命。生命不斷來去，而大地始終以她深沉而永恆的力量給予生命。

透過「巨木之靈」，本作品試圖讓我們瞥見地球古老的意識，並透過沉浸式聲音，感受那股神秘而強大的生命意志。

聆聽方式

敬請於安靜的環境中使用耳機聆聽，以獲得最佳的沉浸效果。專業級耳罩式錄音室監聽耳機能提供最完整的空間感；一般情況下，耳罩式耳機也比入耳式耳機更適合本作品。若沒有專業耳機，早期 iPhone 隨附的有線 EarPods 接上電腦亦可聆聽。

聽眾回饋

「聽完這部作品，覺得好療癒，會感覺置身於電影《阿凡達》的巨木森林裡，很震撼！意猶未盡！」
