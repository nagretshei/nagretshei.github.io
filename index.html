Zoe (Yi-Cheng) Lin is a composer whose work explores immersive sound, spatial perception, technology, and ecological imagination. Her practice moves between acoustic instruments, electronics, spatial audio, and audiovisual media, with particular attention to how sound can shape the perception of space, presence, memory, and dream.

"Maximalism — More is more!"
