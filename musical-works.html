Taroko: A Lullaby of Coastal太魯閣：海風的搖籃曲

A work inspired by Taroko and its coastal landscape, approaching place through the intimate perspective of a lullaby. The music reflects on landscape, memory, and listening, exploring how a sense of place can be transformed into musical sound and imagination.

一首以太魯閣及其海岸地景為靈感的作品，透過搖籃曲般親密的視角切入地方感。音樂映照著地景、記憶與聆聽的關係，探索地方感如何轉化為音樂聲響與想像。

10/2025

Dream Voyager: A Pilgrim of the Infinite夢行者：無垠的朝聖

For solo flute and electronics. The work portrays a journey of consciousness through a lucid dream, moving between states of sleep, heightened awareness, and an imagined departure from the body. Sound and space become a canvas through which the listener travels, while the accompanying visual version extends the work into an audiovisual meditation on the blurred boundaries between reality, illusion, AI-generated imagery, and dreams.

為長笛獨奏與電聲創作。作品描繪意識在清醒夢中的旅程，游走於睡眠、高度清醒與想像中脫離身體的狀態之間。聲音與空間成為聽者穿越的畫布，搭配的視覺版本則將作品延伸為一場探討真實、幻覺、AI 生成影像與夢境之間模糊邊界的視聽冥想。
