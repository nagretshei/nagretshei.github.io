Info

Zoe (Yi-Cheng) Lin is a composer and software engineer with a specialization in digital music and related compositions. Her electronic music has been featured in 21 countries across 48 international music festivals.

Education

  • Ph.D. in Composition — University of Wisconsin–Madison

Experience

  • Former Chief Music Officer, AI music company
  • Full-time composer
  • Assistant Professor (adjunct), National Taiwan Normal University
  • Founder, WomenInMusicTech.com

Specialties

  • Audiovisual / synesthetic electronic music composition
  • 3D immersive electronic music composition & mixing
  • Multichannel ambisonic music projection

Selected Performances & Selections

32+ international showings, including IRCAM (2024), Ars Electronica (2024), the ICMC series, the NYCEMF series, and more. Peer reviewer for ICMC, NIME, and other academic conferences.
