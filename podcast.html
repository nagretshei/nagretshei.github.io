Little Pi's Tech Music Tips ｜ 小皮的聲波速記

Let AI mascot Little Pi give you a fast introduction to the world of electroacoustic music!

讓 AI 吉祥物「小皮」帶你快速認識電聲音樂的世界！

From composers, works, and sound tools to music technology, conferences, and the latest trends, Little Pi delivers one topic worth knowing in about 2–3 minutes of English narration per episode.

從作曲家、作品與聲音工具，到音樂科技、研討會與最新趨勢，小皮每集用約 2～3 分鐘的中文口白，帶你掌握一個值得知道的主題。

Whether you're a composer, musician, student, or a first-time listener to electroacoustic music, join Little Pi for a relaxed, concise journey through the endless possibilities where sound meets technology.

不論你是作曲家、音樂人、學生，還是第一次接觸電聲音樂的聽眾，都可以跟著小皮，用輕鬆、簡潔的方式，探索聲音與科技交會的無限可能。

Electroacoustic music runs deep — Little Pi makes it simple.

電聲音樂很深，小皮幫你說得簡單。

合成器發明前，她用剪刀和膠帶做電視主題曲！無名英雌Delia Derbyshire

全世界最有名的電視主題曲之一，居然是在沒有合成器、沒有電腦的年代，用剪刀和膠帶剪出來的！介紹1963年BBC《超時空博士》主題曲背後的無名英雌Delia Derbyshire，她怎麼手工拼出整首配樂，又為什麼多年沒被列名，直到五十週年特輯才終於被看見。

Before Synthesizers, She Cut a TV Theme With Scissors and Tape: Delia Derbyshire

One of the most famous TV theme tunes in the world was made with no synthesizers and no computers at all. Little Pi introduces Delia Derbyshire, the unsung heroine behind the 1963 Doctor Who theme, who hand-spliced tape using musique concrète techniques and went uncredited for decades.

Ep6 · 2026/09/09

只是很認真聽？她發明了一整套哲學！深度聆聽發明者 Pauline Oliveros

認真聽也能聽出一套哲學？介紹作曲家Pauline Oliveros——她用手風琴加磁帶延遲系統玩了一輩子，還在一次爬進地底蓄水池錄音的經驗中，意外發明了影響全世界的「深度聆聽」練習。

The Woman Who Turned Careful Listening Into a Philosophy: Pauline Oliveros

Can listening carefully become an entire philosophy? Little Pi introduces composer Pauline Oliveros, who spent her life playing accordion through tape delay systems, and accidentally invented Deep Listening after an underground cistern recording session.

The Teacher's Work Just Aired on Canadian Radio! Little Pi's Fan Diary #1

電聲音樂新手必聽！4首改變音樂史的神作大公開

從火車聲音剪成的音樂，到用合成器演奏巴哈，再到專門讓你放空的機場音樂——精選4首橫跨70年、改變電聲音樂走向的關鍵神作。

Four Masterpieces Every Electroacoustic Music Fan Should Know

From music spliced together out of train sounds, to Bach performed on a synthesizer, to music designed to help you zone out in an airport, Little Pi picks four key masterpieces spanning 70 years that changed the direction of electroacoustic music.

NIME是什麼？這是一場「不只發論文，還像演唱會」的國際新樂器發明大會。介紹NIME研討會的由來，以及2026倫敦場次的重點。

The Wildest Instrument-Inventing Conference in the World: NIME Explained

What is NIME? An international conference dedicated to inventing brand-new musical instruments, part research symposium, part live concert. Little Pi covers where NIME came from and what to expect at the 2026 London edition.

Which Tool Should Beginners Learn? ChucK / SuperCollider / Max / PD Guide

Sound Made From Typing?! A Hundred Years of Electroacoustic Music
