第一部全電子配樂電影！《禁忌星球》背後的巴倫夫婦Bebe & Louis Barron

1956年科幻電影《禁忌星球》（Forbidden Planet）的配樂沒有任何傳統樂團，全部是電子聲音，幕後功臣是巴倫夫婦（Bebe & Louis Barron）。這集小皮介紹他們：結婚禮物是一台磁帶錄音機，在紐約格林威治村開了可能是美國第一間電子音樂工作室，幫約翰·凱吉剪接《Williams Mix》；受維納《控制論》啟發，故意讓自製電路過載來產生聲音；片頭字幕因工會問題只能寫「Electronic Tonalities」。

The First All-Electronic Film Score: Bebe and Louis Barron and Forbidden Planet

The 1956 sci-fi film Forbidden Planet has a score with no traditional orchestra at all, only electronic sounds, created by the married couple Bebe and Louis Barron. In this episode, Little Pi tells their story: a tape recorder received as a wedding gift, a studio in Greenwich Village that may have been the first electronic music studio in the United States, engineering John Cage's Williams Mix, and building circuits inspired by Norbert Wiener's Cybernetics that they deliberately overloaded to make sound. Because of a union issue, the film credits could only say Electronic Tonalities.

為什麼混音要用監聽喇叭？監聽喇叭怎麼選、怎麼擺

家裡的音響讓音樂更好聽，監聽喇叭卻要讓你聽見聲音本來的樣子。這集小皮介紹監聽喇叭為什麼追求平直的頻率響應、主動式和被動式的差別、低音單體尺寸要跟著房間大小挑，以及比價錢更要緊的擺位：等邊三角形、高音單體對準耳朵、離後牆遠一點；最後聊聊以「不討好」出名的經典Yamaha NS-10，和混完音多換幾種設備聽的好習慣。

Why Mix on Studio Monitors? How to Choose and Place Them

Home stereos make music sound nicer, but studio monitors let you hear the sound as it really is. In this episode, Little Pi explains why monitors aim for a flat frequency response, the difference between active and passive monitors, why woofer size should match your room, and why placement matters more than price: an equilateral triangle, tweeters at ear height, and some distance from the back wall. Finally, meet the famously unflattering Yamaha NS-10 and learn the habit of checking your mix on several different devices.

不用碰就能演奏！特雷門琴女王克拉拉·羅克摩爾Clara Rockmore

Playing Without Touching: Clara Rockmore, Queen of the Theremin

There's an electronic instrument you can play without touching it at all: the theremin. In this episode, Little Pi introduces Clara Rockmore, queen of the theremin. A violin prodigy admitted to the Saint Petersburg Conservatory at age four, she gave up the violin after developing tendinitis as a teenager. After meeting inventor Leon Theremin in America, she became the instrument's best known player and developed her own aerial fingering technique. Her 1977 album The Art of the Theremin was produced by Robert and Shirleigh Moog, with her sister Nadia Reisenberg on piano.

為什麼每首歌上串流平台都一樣大聲？響度LUFS是什麼

為什麼在串流平台上一首接一首聽，音量卻都差不多？這集小皮從「響度戰爭」講起，介紹依照ITU-R BS.1770標準、模擬人耳聽感的響度單位LUFS：數字通常是負的、越接近0越大聲，歐洲廣播標準EBU R128建議負23 LUFS，Spotify預設播放在負14 LUFS左右；再聊平台的響度正規化，為什麼硬壓得很大聲的歌反而吃虧，以及自己做音樂時該怎麼留峰值空間。

Why Do All Songs Sound Equally Loud on Streaming? What Is LUFS?

Why do songs on streaming platforms all sound about equally loud, one after another? Little Pi starts with the loudness war, then introduces LUFS, the loudness unit defined by the ITU-R BS.1770 standard that imitates how human ears hear. LUFS numbers are usually negative, and closer to zero means louder: the EBU R128 broadcast recommendation targets minus 23 LUFS, while Spotify plays back at around minus 14 LUFS by default. Finally, learn how loudness normalization works, why over-squashed masters lose out, and why you should leave headroom for peaks in your own music.

轉旋鈕、踩踏板，電腦怎麼知道？MIDI CC控制訊號是什麼

MIDI鍵盤上的旋鈕、推桿、踏板一動，電腦裡的聲音就跟著變，背後的功臣就是MIDI CC（控制變更）。這集小皮帶你看懂一則CC訊息的組成——控制編號加上數值，範圍都是0到127；認識最常見的幾個編號：1號調變輪、7號音量、10號左右位置、11號表情、64號延音踏板；最後聊聊在編曲軟體裡畫CC曲線做自動化的用法。

Twist a Knob, Press a Pedal: What Is MIDI CC?

Turn a knob, push a fader, or press a pedal on a MIDI keyboard, and the sound in your computer changes. The hero behind it is MIDI CC (Control Change). In this episode, Little Pi explains what's inside a CC message: a controller number plus a value, both ranging from 0 to 127. Meet the most common numbers: 1 for the modulation wheel, 7 for volume, 10 for pan, 11 for expression, and 64 for the sustain pedal. Finally, learn how drawing CC curves in your music software creates automation.

用電腦寫出第一段音樂！電腦音樂之父馬克斯·馬修斯 Max Mathews

這集小皮介紹被尊稱為「電腦音樂之父」的馬克斯·馬修斯（Max Mathews）。他原本是貝爾實驗室的電機工程師，1957年在IBM 704大型電腦上寫出第一個電腦音樂程式MUSIC，1961年團隊讓電腦合成人聲演唱《黛西貝爾》，這段示範後來還成為《2001太空漫遊》中哈爾九千唱歌橋段的靈感來源。他提出的「單元產生器」積木式設計概念，影響了Csound、SuperCollider、ChucK等後來所有的音樂程式語言。

Writing Music with a Computer for the First Time! Max Mathews, Father of Computer Music

In this episode, Little Pi introduces Max Mathews, honored as the "father of computer music." An electrical engineer at Bell Labs, he wrote MUSIC, the first computer music program, on an IBM 704 mainframe in 1957. In 1961 his colleagues had a computer sing "Daisy Bell" with a synthesized voice, a demo that later inspired the scene where HAL 9000 sings in "2001: A Space Odyssey." His "unit generator" building-block design shaped Csound, SuperCollider, ChucK, and nearly every music programming language since.

Ep13 · 2026/09/16

合成器怎麼「捏」出聲音？減法合成與ADSR包絡

接續上一集的MIDI，這集小皮講合成器怎麼真正「發出聲音」——先用振盪器做出一個泛音超豐富、又吵又刺耳的原始波形，再用濾波器把不要的頻率削掉，最後靠ADSR包絡線（起音、衰減、延持、釋音）決定聲音的個性。同一個波形調一調包絡，就能從打擊聲變成拉弦樂器那種鋪底音色，這套邏輯幾乎所有合成器都通用。

How Does a Synth Actually "Sculpt" Sound? Subtractive Synthesis and the ADSR Envelope

Following up on MIDI, Little Pi explains how a synthesizer actually makes a sound: an oscillator generates a raw, overtone-rich, harsh waveform, a filter carves away the unwanted frequencies, and the ADSR envelope (attack, decay, sustain, release) gives the sound its personality. Tweak the envelope on the exact same waveform and you can go from a drum hit to a lush string-like pad - a logic that applies to nearly every synthesizer out there.

MIDI到底是什麼？不是錄音檔，是一張演奏指令表

MIDI檔案裡其實完全沒有錄到聲音？這集小皮解釋MIDI跟MP3、WAV的根本差別——它記錄的不是聲波，而是一張精細的演奏指令表。從1983年讓不同品牌合成器互相溝通的標準誕生，講到為什麼MIDI檔案這麼小、還能隨時換樂器演奏，以及它四十幾年來一直被遊戲配樂、電影配樂、AI作曲工具使用的原因。

What Is MIDI, Really? Not a Recording, an Instruction Sheet

A MIDI file doesn't actually contain any recorded sound? Little Pi explains the core difference between MIDI and MP3 or WAV: it doesn't store a waveform, it stores a detailed performance instruction sheet. From the 1983 standard that let synthesizers from different brands talk to each other, to why MIDI files are so tiny and can switch instruments instantly, to why it's still used everywhere from game scores to AI music tools over forty years later.
