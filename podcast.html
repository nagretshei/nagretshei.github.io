Little Pi's Tech Music Tips ｜ 小皮的聲波速記

A podcast about electroacoustic and digital music—composers, tools, conferences, and trends—hosted by AI mascot Little Pi. Episodes are narrated in Mandarin Chinese, about 2–3 minutes each.

由AI吉祥物「小皮」主持的電聲音樂科普節目：作曲家、工具、研討會、趨勢，每集約2〜3分鐘中文口白快速導讀。
