Interdisciplinary Works

Projects that cross the boundaries of music, VR/XR, installation art, and AI systems.

2020 – 2021

Diving into Siraya

VR movie directed by Kuan-Yuan Lai. Accepted by ACM SIGGRAPH 2021, BIFAN 2021, and the Kaohsiung Film Festival 2020. Role: sound design, music composition, 3D immersive mixing.

▶ Demo Video

2020

Luminous Path

Light-sound installation for the Taiwan Music Institute, commissioned by NoiseKitchen.tw — a permanent installation along the institute's outdoor corridor. Role: music design, Max/MSP & JavaScript programming.

▶ Demo Video · ▶ Opening Ceremony Video

2018 – 2022

The Breath of the Forest

A dialogue between nature and humans, existing in multiple forms including an electroacoustic version and a WebXR music installation. Eight electroacoustic parts and 22 flute passages, paired with eight 3D video scenes, offering 997,272 possible musical versions.

▶ WebXR Interactive Installation · ▶ Tutorial Video · 🎧 Listen on SoundCloud

2016

Journey into the World of Dimensions

VR game developed with the Unity 3D engine, released as an iOS app titled VArt Journey (since discontinued). Music: a six-movement work for solo saxophone and electroacoustic music.

▶ Concert Video

2021 – present

16th-Century Counterpoint AI Generator

An AI system generating counter-melodies for a given cantus firmus, based on Palestrina's counterpoint style. Third version completed.

▶ Demo Video (1st species) · ▶ Demo Video (2nd species)

2019 – 2020

Four-Part Harmony Generator

A system that generates four-part harmony from Roman numeral input, including secondary dominant chords and modal mixture.
