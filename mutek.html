Zoe Lin is a composer and software engineer. Her “Synesthetic Compositions” use spatial audio and Ambisonics to build immersive, multi-sensory experiences that move between acoustic instruments, electronics and audiovisual media. Her work explores how sound shapes our perception of space, memory, nature and dream, and has been presented at Ars Electronica, IRCAM Forum, ICMC, SEAMUS and NYCEMF.
